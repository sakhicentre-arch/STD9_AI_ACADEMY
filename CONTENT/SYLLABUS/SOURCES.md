## C. Source Hierarchy (for conflict resolution)

When sources disagree, prefer in this order:
1. **GSSTB official portal** (Gujarat textbooks) — `gsstb.gujarat.gov.in`
2. **NCERT official portal** — `ncert.nic.in`
3. **CBSE Academic** — `cbseacademic.nic.in`
4. GCERT, Edustud, GSEB official circulars
5. Established reference sites (Vedantu, BYJU'S, Jagran Josh, Allen) — for chapter lists & solutions only

---

## D. Notes on Source Reliability

- **Official portals** (GSSTB, NCERT, CBSE) are authoritative but sometimes require per-medium/per-subject navigation; no single consolidated PDF catalog.
- **Reference sites** are convenient but reflect a mix of legacy, rationalized (2022-23), and new NCF-SE (2026-27) editions — always note the academic year.
- **Third-party textbook PDFs** (BYJU'S, StudentBro) are scans of older editions; verify the edition year against the official portal before use.
