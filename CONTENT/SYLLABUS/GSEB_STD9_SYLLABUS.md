# Gujarat State Board (GSEB) Std 9 Syllabus

> **Academic Year:** 2025–26
> **Board:** Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSHSEB / GSEB)
> **Textbook Authority:** Gujarat State Board of School Textbooks (GSSTB / GCSTB)
> **Last Researched:** 2025-06-25

---

## 1. Overview

Std 9 is the first year of **secondary education** under the Gujarat Board. It is a school-level (pen-and-paper) examination conducted by schools under GSEB norms. Std 9 is the foundation year for the **Class 10 (SSC) board examination**; the syllabus is set by GSEB and textbooks are published by GSSTB.

The Std 9 curriculum is being progressively aligned with the **National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCFSE 2024 / NCF-SE)**. New textbooks (e.g. the *"Maitri"* series) are being introduced gradually; until full rollout, the existing GSSTB textbooks remain prescribed in many schools.

## 3. Std 9 Subject Structure

Std 9 subjects are grouped into **Languages**, **Core (compulsory)** subjects, and **Optional / additional** subjects.

### 3.1 Languages
| Type | Typical options |
|------|-----------------|
| **First Language** | Gujarati (default in Gujarati medium), or the medium's mother tongue |
| **Second Language** | Hindi / English (depending on medium and school) |
| **Third Language** | Sanskrit / Urdu / Sindhi / Marathi / Tamil / English (varies by medium and school choice) |

### 3.2 Core (Compulsory) Subjects
| # | Subject | Notes |
|---|---------|-------|
| 1 | **Mathematics** | Full-year, both semesters |
| 2 | **Science & Technology** | Theory + practical |
| 3 | **Social Science** | Integrated: History, Geography, Civics (Political Science), Economics |
| 4 | **First Language** (Gujarati / medium language) | Compulsory |
| 5 | **Second Language** | Compulsory |

### 3.3 Optional / Additional Subjects (school-dependent)
- Computer Science / Computer Studies
- Drawing / Art
- Music
- Physical Education
- Work Experience / SUPW
- Vocational subjects

> Exact subject combinations vary by school and medium. Confirm with the school's published Std 9 book list.

## 4. Indicative Chapter / Unit Lists

> The chapter lists below are **indicative** of the GSSTB Std 9 textbooks. Chapter numbering and titles can vary slightly between mediums and between the legacy and new (NCF-aligned) editions. Verify against the official GSSTB textbook PDF for the relevant medium.

### 4.1 Mathematics — Std 9 (GSSTB)
Core areas covered (indicative unit themes):
1. Number System (Real Numbers)
2. Polynomials
3. Coordinate Geometry
4. Linear Equations in Two Variables
5. Euclid's Geometry / Lines and Angles
6. Triangles
7. Quadrilaterals
8. Circle (Circles)
9. Heron's Formula
10. Surface Area and Volume
11. Statistics
12. Introduction to Probability (in some editions)

### 4.2 Science & Technology — Std 9 (GSSTB)
Indicative unit themes (Physics / Chemistry / Biology):
- **Chemistry:** Matter in Our Surroundings · Is Matter Around Us Pure? · Atoms and Molecules · Structure of the Atom
- **Biology:** The Fundamental Unit of Life (Cell) · Tissues · Diversity in Living Organisms
- **Physics:** Motion · Force and Laws of Motion · Gravitation · Work and Energy · Sound
- **Practical Work / Activities:** laboratory-based exercises

### 4.3 Social Science — Std 9 (GSSTB)
Taught as an integrated subject with four strands:
- **History:** Modern world and Indian history themes (e.g. European events, Indian freedom movement, heritage)
- **Geography:** Physical, regional, and resources geography of India and Gujarat
- **Civics / Political Science:** Indian democracy, constitution, governance
- **Economics:** Basic economic concepts, poverty, food security

### 4.4 Languages
- **Gujarati (First Language):** prose, poetry, grammar, composition — from the prescribed GSSTB Gujarati textbook.
- **English (First/Second Language):** prose, poetry, grammar, writing skills.
- **Hindi / Sanskrit / others:** prescribed GSSTB textbooks per medium.

## 6. Research Notes & Caveats

- **NCFSE 2024 transition:** NCERT/GSSTB are rolling out new syllabi and textbooks (e.g. *"Maitri"* series for Maths/Science). Chapter lists above are indicative and may shift as new editions are adopted statewide.
- **Medium differences:** Gujarati medium is the reference edition; chapter numbering in English/Hindi/other mediums can differ slightly.
- **No single consolidated "syllabus PDF" is publicly published by GSEB** for Std 9 in the way board classes publish. The authoritative reference is the **GSSTB textbook itself** (table of contents = syllabus).
- Cross-verify any specific chapter title with the official GSSTB textbook PDF before relying on it for teaching/exams.
