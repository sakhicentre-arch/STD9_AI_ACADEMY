## How GSEB Std 9 Books Work

- GSEB Std 9 textbooks are published by the **Gujarat State Board of School Textbooks (GSSTB)** — the sole authority for state-board textbooks.
- Books are first published in **Gujarati medium**, then in **Hindi, English, Marathi, Sindhi, Urdu, Sanskrit, and Tamil** mediums.
- Official distribution is through GSSTB's online portal and selected textbook depots. Some third-party sites (StudentBro, GSEB Solutions, BYJU'S, SelfStudys) host downloadable PDFs.
- The curriculum is transitioning to the **NCFSE 2024 / NCF-SE** framework; a new textbook series (e.g. *"Maitri"*) is being introduced. Until full rollout, the existing GSSTB editions remain prescribed.

## Research Notes & Caveats

- **Per-medium book lists** are not consolidated into a single public catalog page on the GSSTB site; the portal requires navigating per standard/medium/subject. Exact ISBNs/prices should be confirmed on the portal or via the school's book list.
- **"First/Second/Third Language"** assignment depends on the student's medium of instruction — the same physical book title can serve different language-slots in different mediums.
- **NCF-SE transition:** New *"Maitri"* series textbooks are replacing legacy editions subject-by-subject. Schools may be on either edition during 2025-26.
- **Third-party PDFs** (BYJU'S, StudentBro, SelfStudys) are convenient but should be cross-checked against the official GSSTB portal for the latest authorized edition.
