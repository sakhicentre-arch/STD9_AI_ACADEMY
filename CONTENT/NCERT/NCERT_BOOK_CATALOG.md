---

## How NCERT Class 9 Books Work

- NCERT publishes official textbooks for Classes I–XII in **Hindi, English, and Urdu** mediums.
- All NCERT textbooks are available **free as PDF** from the official portal (`ncert.nic.in/textbook.php`).
- Each book has a unique textbook **code** (e.g. `iemh1` for Class 9 Maths English). Individual chapters are downloadable as separate PDFs.
- **Curriculum transition in progress:** Under the **NCFSE 2024 / NCF-SE** framework, NCERT is releasing **new editions** for 2026-27 — e.g. *"Ganita Manjari"* (Maths), *"Exploration"* (Science), *"Kaveri"* (English). Until full rollout, the 2025-26 books below remain prescribed.

## 9. Research Notes & Caveats

- **Rationalization:** NCERT rationalized syllabi (2022–23 onwards) dropped some chapters/topics from older editions. The 2025-26 prescribed books reflect these cuts.
- **2026-27 new editions:** *"Ganita Manjari"* (Maths), *"Exploration"* (Science), *"Kaveri"* (English) are part of the new NCF-SE rollout. Chapter counts above apply to the **current 2025-26** editions and will change.
- **Chapter counts** are best verified directly on the official NCERT textbook page (each book's table of contents is authoritative).
- **Languages:** NCERT also publishes textbooks for other Class 9 subjects (e.g. Health & Physical Education, Art, vocational modules) — these are supplementary and not core to the academic stream.
